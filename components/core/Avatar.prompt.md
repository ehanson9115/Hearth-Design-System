**Avatar** — royal-tinted initials chip (or image). Use in activity rows, tables, transcripts.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Maria Alvarez" />
<Avatar name="Jordan Chen" size="lg" />
<Avatar src="/photo.jpg" name="Sam Ortiz" />
```

Sizes `sm` (24) · `md` (28, default) · `lg` (36). Initials are the first two words of `name`.
